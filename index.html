A few useful answers

Before you get started.

Does MicMoment find the best moments automatically?

Not yet. It splits the video section you provide into clips of your selected duration. Use the optional start and end times to focus on a particular part of the recording.

Can I upload an audio-only podcast?

Not currently. MicMoment processes video recordings, such as MP4 or MOV files, rather than audio-only files.

Can I create vertical clips for TikTok and YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Choose the vertical 9:16 format before creating your clips. You can also keep the original shape or choose landscape or square.

Can I process more than one recording?

Yes. Select multiple video files and MicMoment will upload and process them as a batch. The resulting clips are available as individual downloads.

Can I add captions?

Yes. Open the caption settings, choose your language and style, and enable caption generation before creating your clips.
